### Findings

#### 1. Wall lattice points are misclassified by round-off: asymmetric grids — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Domains.jl:207` (lattice) and `:214` (classification), with `get_rectangular_domain`'s closed-interval
`is_interior` (`Domains.jl:50-57`).
The lattice is built as `canvas_mins + (I-1)·dx` with `canvas_mins = mins − N_ghost·dx`. The point that should
sit exactly on `maxs` comes out as `maxs + O(ulp)` for many spacings and is then classified as a **ghost**
(boundary particle, frozen/BC-driven), while the point on `mins` stays interior.
- Scenario: `[-1, 1]`, `Ns = 30` (`dx = 2/30`, what `build_particle_grid` computes): 30 interior particles instead
  of 31, the interior ends at x = 0.9333 while it starts at −1.0. Same for `[0, 0.7]`, `dx = 0.07`. In 2-D a whole
  wall row/column is lost.
- Frequency (`probe_lattice_freq.jl`): of `Ns = 10…400`, 75 (Float64) / 109 (Float32) values lose a wall point on
  `[-1, 1]` and `[-0.5, 0.5]`; 24/31 on `[0, 1]` (e.g. Ns = 20, 22, 25, 30, 46 on [-1,1]).
- Effect: a symmetric problem runs on an asymmetric cloud; at a Dirichlet/outflow wall the wall particle is evolved
  on one side and frozen on the other; `N_interior` is not `prod(Ns .+ 1)`.
- Evidence: `probe_grid.jl` ("lattice points on the boundary…", 2 failures), `probe_lattice_freq.jl`.
- Fix: generate coordinates from the physical box, `geom.mins[d] + k·dx` for `k = -N_ghost … Ns + N_ghost`, and
  snap `k == Ns` to `maxs` exactly; or classify with a tolerance (`is_interior` with `sqrt(eps)·dx` slack).

#### 2. Mixed periodicity: ghosts near the periodic seam get left/right tags — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Domains.jl:59-79` (`tag_func`).
In 2-D the tag is the nearest of the four edge *lines*, including the edges of a periodic axis, which have no
boundary. For a channel periodic in x with walls in y, every ghost below/above the domain whose x is closer to 0
or 1 than its y is to the wall gets tag 1 or 2 instead of 3/4.
- Scenario: `get_rectangular_domain(Float64, (0,0), (1,1); is_periodic = (true, false), bc_map = Dict(3 => OutflowBC(), 4 => OutflowBC()))`,
  `dx = 0.05`, `R = 2.5dx`: 29 of the 120 ghosts are tagged 1/2 (e.g. `(0.0, −0.15) → 1`, `(0.85, −0.15) → 2`).
  These tags are not in the map, so no boundary condition touches those particles: the stage predictor
  (`TimeSteppers.jl:366`) keeps their previous state, i.e. they act as a silent Dirichlet strip at both ends of
  each wall, inconsistent with the outflow wall. The fraction shrinks like `(R+dx)/L` but never vanishes.
- Evidence: `probe_tags_mixed.jl`.
- Fix: in `tag_func`, ignore the distances to periodic axes (set them to `Inf`), or tag by the axis that is
  actually violated (`pos[d] < mins[d]` / `> maxs[d]`) and only fall back to the nearest edge at corners.
  Optionally warn once in `apply_boundary_conditions!` (or the grid constructor) if some boundary particle's tag is
  not in `bc_map`.

#### 3. `math_min` / `math_max` are not exact: limiters switch off reconstruction, `Inf` gives `NaN` — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Utils.jl:82-85`, used by `limiter_phi` (`src/Interpolation/Limiter.jl:35-51`), the MOOD DMP bounds
(`src/TimeStepping/MOOD.jl:153-167, 254-256`) and the adaptivity clamp (`src/Grid/Adaptivity.jl:379, 437, 501`).
`0.5(a + b ∓ |a − b|)` cancels catastrophically when `|a| ≫ |b|`, overflows when `a + b` overflows, and is `NaN`
for an infinite argument. Observed (`probe_utils.jl`): `math_min(1.0, Inf) = NaN`, `math_max(-Inf, 1.0) = NaN`,
`math_min(1.0, 1e-17) = 0.0`, `math_min(1f0, 1f8) = 0f0`, `math_max(3f38, 3f38) = Inf32`,
`math_min(0.1, 0.3) = 0.10000000000000002`.
- Consequence in the limiters: `limiter_phi(BJ/minmod, r)` returns **0 instead of 1** once `r > 2^24 ≈ 1.7e7`
  (Float32) or `r > 2^53` (Float64); superbee returns 0 instead of 2. `r = (u_max − f_i)/δ` is only guarded by
  `|δ| > 1e-12` (`Limiter.jl:72`), so in Float32 an interface increment `δ < 6e-8·(u_max − f_i)` — e.g. a nearly
  flat gradient next to a varying neighbour — zeroes that component's slope instead of leaving it unlimited:
  `_update_phi(BarthJespersenLimiter(:hard), 1, δ = 1f-8, f_i = 0, u ∈ [-1, 1])` returns `φ = 0`
  (`probe_grid.jl`). In Float64 it needs a large dynamic range (dimensional variables, δ ≈ 1e-12 vs bounds ≈ 1e4).
- DMP bounds and the adaptivity clamp are off by rounding (bounds can be loosened to 0 for widely different
  magnitudes; the clamp can return a value 1 ulp outside `[lo, hi]`).
- The existing test (`unit_core.jl`, "Parameter casting & branchless math") only uses small integers, where the
  formula is exact.
- Fix: `ifelse(a > b, a, b)` / `ifelse(a < b, a, b)` (branchless, exact, GPU-friendly), or plain `max/min` (also
  branchless for floats; differs only in NaN/±0 handling).

#### 4. Adaptivity inserts interior particles outside the domain — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Adaptivity.jl:309` (`_detect_events`, insertion branch) and `:208` (`_has_adapt_candidates`).
The merge branch requires `!isb[j]`, the insertion branch does not: a gap between an interior particle and a
**ghost** triggers an insertion at the midpoint, and `_apply_topology!` (`:591-597`) flags the new particle
interior (`is_boundary = false`, `tag = 0`) wherever the midpoint is.
- Scenario (`probe_adapt.jl`): 1-D `[0, 1]`, `dx = 0.01`, interior positions scaled by 0.992 (the ghosts do not
  move: `_stage_predictor!` keeps boundary positions), uniform state. `adapt_particles!` inserts one particle at
  x = 1.001, **outside Ω, flagged interior**. It is then evolved by the scheme, gets a remap volume although no
  quadrature point of its own lies inside, and it is never reached by the boundary conditions. Any moving grid
  whose near-wall particles drift away from the (fixed) ghost layer can do this.
- Fix: skip insertions with `isb[j]` (as for merges), or require `geom.is_interior(mid)` (host side; the kernel
  pre-check can stay a superset).

#### 5. `adapt_particles!` mutates the grid before it fails on a reserved capacity — low — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Adaptivity.jl:105-112`, `:512` (in-place write-back of each remap), `:578` (`ensure_capacity!` only in
`_apply_topology!`).
On a host grid frozen by `reserve!`, the remaps of all events first rewrite survivors' `U` and `V` in place, and
only then `_apply_topology!` calls `ensure_capacity!` and throws `CapacityError`. The new particles' content is
lost and the grid is left in a non-conservative intermediate state.
- Scenario (`probe_reserve_adapt.jl`): 1-D periodic, a removed particle, `reserve!(pg; limit = memory_estimate(pg).bytes + 1, tolerance = 0)`,
  `adapt_particles!` → `CapacityError` (as documented), but 16 particles already changed and `total_mass` dropped
  from 2.0 to 1.974 (−1.3 %). The `MemoryBudget.jl` header promises that "the run either fits from the start or
  stops with a clear error"; a caller that catches the error (e.g. to continue without adaptivity) continues on a
  corrupted state. (The device path is fine: the host copy absorbs the remap and `_upload_particles!` checks the
  capacity before copying.)
- Fix: compute `N_new` from the events and call `ensure_capacity!(hg, N_new)` (and, on a device grid,
  `ensure_capacity!(pg, N_new)`) before the first `_remap_event!`.

#### 6. `pg.reorder(pg)` fails once the grid buffers have grown — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Reordering.jl:94-104` with `ReorderData` (`ParticleGrids.jl:106-111`, `:440-450`).
`Base.permute!(v, p)` needs `length(p) == length(v)`. `rd.permutation` is sized `N` at construction; after any
`ensure_capacity!` (every net insertion of the adaptivity) both arrays have capacity `> N`, and only `p[1:N]` is
initialised: the tail holds `resize!` garbage. Result: `BoundsError` (probe: "attempt to access 132-element
Growable … at index [100, 81, …]"); with lengths that differ (e.g. after `reserve!`, which shrinks the grid
buffers but not the host permutation) a `DimensionMismatch`; if the garbage happened to be valid indices, the tail
would be silently scrambled.
- The docs (`docs/src/particle_grids/overview.md:57`) present `pg.reorder(pg)` as something to call between time
  steps, and `test/time_integration.jl:114` says "then reorder for locality" but never calls it; the suite only
  reorders inside the constructor (lengths equal), so this is untested.
- Evidence: `probe_grid.jl` ("reorder after growth").
- Fix: permute only the active range, e.g. `permute!(view(parent(x), 1:N), view(p, 1:N))` for every array (with a
  scratch copy), and size the check `issorted(view(p, 1:N))` accordingly. Also bump `meta.geometry_version` (or
  document that `pg.neighbor(pg)` must follow, as the docs do).

#### 7. 1-D/3-D boxes give every ghost tag 1; maps with other tags are silently ignored — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Domains.jl:62-79` (`D == 2` only), documented in `docs/src/particle_grids/domains.md:23`.
In 1-D both walls get tag 1, so `bc_map = Dict(1 => FixedDirichlet(), 2 => OutflowBC())` applies Dirichlet on
**both** walls and the tag-2 entry is dead, without any warning. HyperCloud's `bgk_1d_sod` and `euler_1d_sod`
examples use exactly this map with the comments "# Left Boundary" / "# Right Boundary", i.e. they believe the right
wall is an outflow. (Sod at t = 0.2 does not reach the walls, so their results are unaffected so far.)
- Evidence: `probe_grid.jl` ("1-D rectangular domain…": left tags `[1]`, right tags `[1]`).
- Fix: tag 1-D walls 1 (left) / 2 (right) and 3-D faces 1–6 (natural extension of the 2-D scheme); at least warn
  when `bc_map` contains tags that no particle carries, or when boundary particles carry tags not in the map
  (also covers finding 2).

#### 8. Volume quadrature: the stated orders do not hold on bounded or jittered clouds — medium — CONFIRMED
`src/Grid/Volumes.jl:15-22` (header: Shepard "second order on quasi-uniform clouds"; MLS "Σ V_i q(x_i) = ∫ q exactly
for polynomials q of degree ≤ P … a quadrature of order P+1"), `:52` ("A consistent quadrature on any cloud"),
`:204-209` (renormalisation over *all* particles, ghosts included).
- Non-periodic box `[0,1]`, lattice, `f = x²` (`probe_grid.jl`): Shepard error 7.8e-2 → 8.9e-3 for h = 1/20 … 1/160,
  **EOC 1.0**; MLS(2) error 1.4e-3 → 1.1e-4, **EOC 1.1**. The ghost particles outside Ω get full Shepard volumes,
  so Σ of the interior volumes is short by 4.4 % at h = 1/160 (EOC 0.9); `total_mass` therefore includes the
  (BC-driven) ghost content. For MLS the negative ghost volumes are clamped to zero before the renormalisation
  (acknowledged in a test comment, `unit_schemes.jl:604-607`), which costs the exactness.
- Jittered periodic cloud (no ghosts), `f = 1 + sin πx + x²` (`probe_shepard_periodic.jl`): Shepard does not
  converge: error ≈ 3e-3 at 30 % jitter for N = 40 … 320 (EOC 0.4, 0.4, −0.3), ≈ 1e-4 at 10 % jitter.
- The tests only assert `err_mls < 5e-3` and `err_mls < err_shepard` on the bounded cloud.
- Suggest: state the actual orders in the docstrings (Shepard: first order with ghost layers, non-convergent on
  jittered clouds; MLS: P+1 only on periodic clouds); consider giving ghosts zero volume, or excluding them from the
  renormalisation, if `Σ V U` is meant to be the content of Ω.

#### 9. Shock-band exclusion radius is smaller than the remap reach in 2-D/3-D — low — SUSPECTED
`src/Grid/Adaptivity.jl:278` uses `(4R)^2` to mark "particles whose order was lowered, plus everything their remap
could touch", while `_event_spacing`'s own analysis (`:244-257`) says a remap reads/changes particles up to
`R(1 + 2√D) + dx` from its centre (3.83R + dx in 2-D, 4.46R + dx in 3-D), and an event centre lies within R/2 of
its starting particle. So an event started just outside 4R of a reduced-order particle can still change that
particle's state/volume in 2-D/3-D. Reasoning only, not reproduced. Fix: use `_event_spacing(pg)` (or
`R(1 + 2√D) + dx + R/2`) for the shock band as well.

#### 10. The default suite runs every threaded path on one thread — low — CONFIRMED (by reading)
`test/runtests.jl:94`: `threads = max(1, Threads.nthreads() ÷ jobs)` → with `-t 8` and 5 groups every child process
gets 1 thread (CI and the command in CLAUDE.md). With one thread `@batch` runs inline and `@threads` has one task,
so the Polyester-vs-Threads parity (`unit_backends.jl:57`), the BC backend comparison and the allocation tests never
exercise concurrent execution, and thread-count independence is not tested anywhere. My `probe_threads.jl`
(2-D box, outflow + Dirichlet, MUSCL3/VK/MOOD, `-t 1` vs `-t 4`) is bitwise identical, so I found no actual race;
this is a coverage gap. Suggest: give at least the `backends-*` groups ≥ 2 threads (e.g. `max(2, …)`), or add a
two-process thread-count check like the probe.

#### 11. A pipeline assertion that cannot fail — low — CONFIRMED (by reading)
`test/pipeline_tests.jl:215`: `@test e1 < max(2e0, 1e-6)`, i.e. the absolute discrete-mass error of the adaptive
moving run must be below 2, while the total mass of the Gaussian IC is ≈ 0.18 (`IC_width = 0.1`). `e0` is computed
and unused. Suggest bounding `e1` relative to the mass (and against `e0`), e.g. `e1 < 1e-10 * mass` if the
adaptivity is meant to be exact on a moving grid with re-estimated volumes, or a measured tolerance.

#### 12. CLAUDE.md says Julia 1.10 is supported, the package requires 1.12 — low — CONFIRMED
`CLAUDE.md:65` ("Supports **Julia 1.10** (`[compat] julia = "1.10"`, tested on GitHub)") vs `Project.toml`
`julia = "1.12"` since commit e89da77 "Dropped Julia 1.10 compatibility". The HyperCloudDev CLAUDE.md also says
"HyperCloudCore also tests Julia 1.10 there". The invariant (no 1.11+ features) is now moot; the docs should be
updated so contributors don't constrain themselves (or the compat restored if 1.10 is still wanted).

#### 13. `device_grid(Array, host_grid)` aliases instead of copying — low — CONFIRMED (by reading)
`src/DeviceAdapt.jl:61-76, 85-86`: for `AT === Array` and a host grid, `adapt(Array, a)` returns `a` itself and
`nd_host = pg.neighbor` is reused, so the "copy" shares every array (and `pg.reorder`, `pg.geometry`) with the
original; only `meta` is new. Internally it is only called on device grids, but the docstring says "A copy of
`pg`". Either document "shares the arrays when they already have the requested type" or `copy` in that case.

### Minor issues

- `src/Types.jl:75`: `MLSWeightFunction` says the functor takes the radial distance `r`; all implementations and
  the neighbour search pass `r²` (`NeighborLogic.jl:322`).
- `src/Grid/MLSWeightFunctions.jl`: `InverseWeightFunction` ignores `alpha` (docstring calls it a "scaling
  parameter"); `fast_exp_accurate` is called "highly accurate" but `1/(Taylor₄ of e^y)` is 0.4 % off at `alpha = 1`
  and 23 % at `alpha = 3` at the cutoff (harmless as a kernel, misleading as a description);
  `ExponentialWeightFunction(1.0, 0.25f0)` is a `MethodError` (both arguments must share `T`).
- `docs/src/particle_grids/overview.md:24`: "runs a neighbour search, reorders …, searches again" — the constructor
  reorders first and searches once (`ParticleGrids.jl:275-277`).
- `FixedDirichlet`/`OutflowBC` docstrings: the fallback/restored state is the *current* `pg.rhos`, not the
  "initial original state" (equal only for boundary particles nobody else writes).
- `src/Grid/Reordering.jl:75-85`: Float64 literals (`65535.0`, `1023.0`) in host code; 10-bit Morton keys in 3-D
  lose locality beyond 1024 points per axis.
- `src/Grid/Adaptivity.jl` `_dmp_repair!`: `abs(excess) <= eps(T)` is an absolute threshold (scale-dependent; in
  Float32 with small volumes it drops a real, if round-off-sized, excess); `fill!(view(lmap, 1:N), 0)` per event is
  O(N·events); argument validation by `@assert` in `ParticleAdaptivity`.
- `src/Builders/Builders.jl:93-94`: `parse_bc` `eval`s configuration strings in the package module (trusted configs
  only); tags from text configs (`"1"`) fail on the `Dict{Int,…}` conversion with an unhelpful error. (HyperCloud's
  `configuration.md` says `:Grid_bc_map` is ignored, but `parse_bc` does read `:bc_map` — cross-repo doc mismatch.)
- `src/Builders/domains.jl:110-111`: `/ 2.0` Float64 literals (converted later, harmless); the spherical domain
  silently uses the x extent for the radius.
- `get_time_step` silently returns `Inf` if every interior fit fails, which a driver loop turns into one step to
  `t_end`; a warning would help.
- `param2uvec((1, 2.0))` gives an `SVector{2, Real}` (abstract element type).

### Verdict

The engine core of this area is in good shape: the neighbour search (cell list as counting sort, deterministic
order, row-/slot-major layouts) matched brute force in every configuration I tried (1–3-D, periodic/mixed/none,
Float32/Float64, jitter), results are bitwise independent of the thread count, the memory budget accounts for every
non-empty buffer, a Float32 run through all builders with adaptivity works and stays conservative, and the suite
passes (838/838). The defects are at the geometry edges: round-off in the wall classification (asymmetric grids for
~20 % of resolutions), boundary tagging (mixed periodicity, 1-D/3-D) that silently leaves boundary particles
without their condition, adaptivity insertions next to the ghost layer that create interior particles outside Ω,
the documented `pg.reorder` that breaks after growth, and the inexact `math_min/max` that disable limiting in
Float32. None of these is caught by the current tests; each has a probe ready to become a regression test.
Findings: 0 high, 7 medium (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8), 6 low (5, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13); 12 confirmed, 1 suspected (9).

### Findings

#### F1 (high, CONFIRMED) — `IMEX_ARS222_Tableau` is not ARS(2,2,2): first order on coupled problems
`src/TimeStepping/ButcherTableaus.jl:184-191`.
The published ARS(2,2,2) has three stages with an explicit first stage (`Y₁ = Uⁿ`), explicit weights
`b̃ = (δ, 1-δ, 0)` applied to `F(Uⁿ), F(Y₂), F(Y₃)`. The code drops the first stage and keeps the explicit
coefficients, so the explicit weights now act on `F(Y_γ), F(Y_1)` where `Y_γ = Uⁿ + Δt γ S(Y_γ)` is already an
implicit stage. Each part alone is 2nd order, but the coupling conditions fail: `b̃ᵀc − ½ = 1.0`,
`bᵀc̃ − ½ = g² − ½ = −0.41`.
- Scenario: any IMEX run with `:ARS222` (HyperCloud's kinetic/BGK examples `bgk_1d_sod.jl`, `burgers_kinetic.jl`
  and the inbuilt `ARS222MUSCL2` methods) is first order in time wherever transport and source interact;
  in the stiff limit its error is ~100× that of SSP2332 at the same Δt.
- Evidence: `probe_tableaus.jl` (coupled o2 residual 1.0, EOC 1.02/1.05), `probe_imex_stepper.jl` through the real
  stepper (err 5.56e-3 → 2.69e-3 for Δt = 1/40 → 1/80, EOC 1.05), `probe_imex_stiff.jl` (λ = 1e6: 2.4e-2 vs 3e-8 for
  the 3-stage form).
- Why the suite passes: "Implicit stage: RHS from the solve" (`test/unit_schemes.jl:266`) has a zero explicit part
  (constant state), so the coupling never enters, and its tolerance `5e-3` is not an order check; the tableau test in
  `unit_core.jl:377` checks only `Σb = Σb̃ = 1`.
- Fix: the three-stage form `A = [0 0 0; 0 γ 0; 0 1-γ γ]`, `Ã = [0 0 0; γ 0 0; δ 1-δ 0]`, `b = (0, 1-γ, γ)`,
  `b̃ = (δ, 1-δ, 0)`, `c = c̃ = (0, γ, 1)` (verified in `probe_tableaus.jl`: coupled residual 1e-16, EOC 2.01). Costs one
  more explicit RHS per step; update the docs table ("2 stages").

#### F2 (high, CONFIRMED) — `IMEX_PRSSP3_Tableau` is inconsistent: implicit part first order, wrong node, wrong stiff limit
`src/TimeStepping/ButcherTableaus.jl:193-207`.
Row 3 of the implicit matrix sums to `1.137`, not the declared `c₃ = 0.5`; the implicit part alone fails the
order-2 condition (`bᵀc − ½ = 0.42`). Docstring and docs claim "3rd-order"; with three stages, `b = (1/6, 1/6, 2/3)`
and `c = (γ, 1-γ, ½)` an implicit order 3 is impossible anyway (`bᵀc² = 1/3` needs `γ ∈ {0, 1}`), which is why
Pareschi–Russo's 3-stage scheme SSP3(3,3,2) is order 2 and their order-3 scheme SSP3(4,3,3) has four stages.
- Scenario: `:PRSSP3` (recommended in `docs/src/time_integration/tableaus.md` for stiff relaxation sources; used by
  HyperCloud's inbuilt kinetic methods) gives first-order results and an O(Δt) error of ~15 % at Δt = 1/40 in the
  stiff limit (it does not converge to the relaxed solution as ε → 0 with fixed Δt).
- Evidence: `probe_tableaus.jl` (node error 0.64, sep. o2 0.42, EOC 1.03/1.05), `probe_imex_stepper.jl` (EOC 1.05),
  `probe_imex_stiff.jl` (λ = 1e4…1e6: errors 0.17 → 0.096 and 0.15 → 0.076).
- Fix: replace by PR SSP3(3,3,2) (`γ = 1 − 1/√2`, `A = [γ 0 0; 1-2γ γ 0; ½-γ 0 γ]`, explicit SSP-RK3, `b = b̃`;
  verified: coupled residual 0, EOC 1.99) and call it order 2, or implement SSP3(4,3,3) for order 3. Add an
  order-condition test (incl. `c == rowsum(A)`) for every IMEX tableau.

#### F3 (medium, CONFIRMED) — implicit sources with an explicit tableau are silently dropped; the warning says otherwise
`src/TimeStepping/TimeSteppers.jl:220-222` (warning), `:349-356`, `:373-381`.
With an explicit tableau `a = 0` and `b = 0`, so `K_I` is computed (`_stage_implicit_rhs!`) but multiplied by zero
in every stage and in the final update. The warning "the implicit solve is skipped (a[i,i] = 0)" suggests the source
is still applied explicitly.
- Scenario: `GeneralRKTimeStepper(eq, m, MOOD(), (ImplDecay(3.0),), RK3_SSP_Tableau(Float64))`, one step with
  `dt = 0.1` from `u = 1`: result `1.000000000000000` (expected ≈ 0.7408).
- Evidence: `probe_implicit_with_explicit_tableau.jl`.
- Fix: throw an `ArgumentError`, or move implicit sources into the explicit part (`b̃`) when the tableau has no
  implicit part, and say so in the warning.

#### F4 (medium, CONFIRMED) — MUSCL divergence cache and CFL degree ignore the neighbour-count safeguard
`src/Interpolation/MUSCL.jl:22-32` (`_divergence_index`), `:398` (`cached` needs `div_idx == _full_div_idx`),
`src/Interpolation/MUSCL_hooks.jl:15-18` (`_divergence_degree`, `_cfl_slot_weights`).
The cache (`div_weights`, `dweights`) and the CFL degree are built for `_full_div_idx`, but `_divergence_index`
lowers the degree on small stencils. In the repo's default 1-D configuration (MUSCL3, R = 2.5Δx, 4 neighbours) every
particle fits degree 2 while the cache is degree 3: the cache is never hit (every RHS assembles and factorises a
moment matrix per particle; the streamed/aligned paths fall back to dynamic weights) and `get_time_step` sums the
weights of the degree-3 fit the scheme never uses.
- Scenario: MUSCL3, R = 2.5Δx: Σ|c_ij|Δx = 1.500 (CFL) vs 0.644 (used), so the step is 2.3× smaller than the
  configured CFL implies; MUSCL5, R = 3.5Δx: 1.833 vs 1.167 (1.6×); MUSCL5, R = 2.5Δx: 1.475 vs 1.500, i.e. the step
  is slightly *larger* than the configured CFL (the cached degree-5 fit is under-determined there: 4 neighbours,
  6 unknowns, it only exists through the Tikhonov retry).
- Evidence: `probe_cache_degree.jl`.
- Fix: decide the full divergence degree from the stencil once (per particle, or the minimum over the cloud) and
  build `div_weights`/`dweights` and `_divergence_degree` for that degree; or cache per particle the degree that
  `_divergence_index` returns at full order.

#### F5 (medium, CONFIRMED by inspection) — WENO smoothness indicators ignore the higher derivatives (code ≠ docs)
`src/Interpolation/WENO.jl:96-103`, `:140-147`.
`resC`/`resS` are truncated to the `D` first derivatives before the indicator loop, so `k <= D` always holds and
the `Δx⁴` branch is dead; `IS` is `Σ (∂u)² Δx²` only. `docs/src/spatial/weno.md` defines
`IS = Σ_{β≤D} (Dᵝu)² Δx² + Σ_{β>D} (Dᵝu)² Δx⁴`, and the constructor requires `order ≥ 2` "for second derivatives".
- Scenario: a smooth extremum vs a kink with the same gradient magnitude get the same weights; the "order ≥ 2"
  requirement buys nothing for the weights.
- Fix: compute the indicators from the full `resC_raw`/`resS_raw` (all `b_len` entries), keep `resC[d]` for the
  divergence. Also: `e_tol = 1e-12` (line 82) is absolute, see F7.

#### F6 (low, CONFIRMED) — `AlignedRusanovFlux`: "both sides evaluate the same flux" is false on irregular clouds
`src/Interpolation/FluxFunctions.jl:88`, `src/Interpolation/MUSCL.jl:328-334`, `docs/src/spatial/muscl.md:100`.
The orientation of axis `d` on slot `(i, j)` is `sign(c_ij,d)` from `i` and `sign(c_ji,d)` from `j`; the two sides agree
only if `sign(c_ji,d) = −sign(c_ij,d)`. Only the choice of *where* the aligned sorting applies (`below`) is symmetric.
- Evidence: `probe_aligned_symmetry.jl`: same-sign weights on 383/7846 (pair, axis) of a 2-D cloud with jitter 0.2,
  374/7484 with jitter 0.4 (K = 1, R = 2.5Δx), 1952/15106 for the degree-3 fit at R = 3.5Δx (0.4–2.5 % of the
  `Σ|c|` mass); 0 in 1-D and on lattices. On those slots `i` and `j` use opposite dissipation signs, an extra
  pair defect on top of the face-vector mismatch. The LED property of each particle's own update is unaffected.
- Fix: correct the docstrings/docs, or orient by a pair-symmetric quantity (e.g. `sign(c_ij,d − c_ji,d)`) if
  pairwise consistency is wanted.

#### F7 (low, CONFIRMED) — absolute thresholds make the limiters (and WENO weights) scale dependent
`src/Interpolation/Limiter.jl:72` (`abs(recon_m) > T(1e-12)`), `src/Interpolation/WENO.jl:82` (`e_tol = 1e-12`),
`src/Interpolation/FluxFunctions.jl:133`, `Limiter.jl:161-183` (1e-9/1e-12 distance and slope thresholds).
- Scenario: a box of height 1e-13 (e.g. the tail of a discrete velocity distribution in BGK runs): BJ and VK
  (hard) leave it completely unlimited, 16 % relative overshoot, identical to `NoLimiter`; at height 1 or 1e-9 the
  overshoot is 0.
- Evidence: `probe_limiter_scale.jl`.
- Fix: scale the tolerance with the local range, e.g. `abs(recon_m) > eps(T) * max(abs(f_i[m]), u_max[m] - u_min[m])`
  (and `e_tol` relative to `Σ (Δu)²` of the stencil).

#### F8 (low, CONFIRMED) — `math_min`/`math_max` lose precision; used for the MOOD DMP bounds
`src/Utils.jl:82-83`, used in `src/TimeStepping/MOOD.jl:149-170, 252-256` and `Limiter.jl:35-51`.
`½(a + b ∓ |a − b|)` has an absolute error of ~eps·max(|a|, |b|): `math_min(1f3, 1f-3) = 9.77f-4` (2.3 % off),
`math_min(1e20, 1) = 0`, `math_max(-1e20, -1) = 0` (both Float64). The DMP extrema of a component spanning several
orders of magnitude in a neighbourhood (density/pressure near vacuum, Float32 in particular) are then wrong by more
than a small `δ`, which can flag (or pass) the wrong particles. Also a NaN neighbour propagates differently from
`min`/`max`.
- Fix: use `min`/`max` (`ifelse`-based, branch free on GPUs as well); the limiter already does in `_local_extrema`.

#### F9 (low, CONFIRMED by inspection) — Float64 constant in a kernel path
`src/TimeStepping/MOOD.jl:245, 273`: `near_sq = (_U2_RATIO_RADIUS * h)^2` with `_U2_RATIO_RADIUS = 1.5` promotes
the MOODu2 check to Float64 for Float32 grids (violates the "no Float64 literals in numerics" rule; Float64 on
consumer GPUs is slow or unsupported). Fix: `T(_U2_RATIO_RADIUS)`.

#### F10 (low, SUSPECTED) — Central/Upwind neighbour-count check ignores the intercept
`src/Interpolation/Central.jl:198-206`, `src/Interpolation/Upwind.jl:93-101`: the divergence fit
(`_mls_divergence`) has `basis_count(D, q) + 1` unknowns, the check requires only `basis_count(D, q)` neighbours
(MUSCL's `_divergence_index` uses `+ 1`). A particle with exactly `basis_count` neighbours (boundary corners,
sparse clouds) gets the Tikhonov-regularised rank-deficient fit instead of a lower degree. On a symmetric
2-neighbour lattice stencil (1-D, R = 1.5Δx) the regularised Central2 fit gives exactly the Central1 result
(`probe_central_starved.jl`), so it is harmless there; one-sided/irregular starved stencils not checked.

### Test coverage

Well tested: the aligned dissipation's first-order coefficients (several configurations, cached and dynamic weights),
the moment correction, the SSP coefficients and canonical Shu–Osher form, the MOOD substep checkpoints (recording
criterion, nicely direct), streamed vs buffered divergence, the explicit tableaus' order conditions (to 1e-13) plus
observed order on an ODE, MOOD kernel passes, MLS divergence zero-sum and odd-degree logic, MUSCL EOC on lattices
(orders 2–5, ± MOOD), Lagrangian exactness.

Missing or weak:
- **IMEX tableaus**: only `Σb = 1`, `Σb̃ = 1` and `rowsum(Ã) = c̃` are checked; no `rowsum(A) = c`, no implicit or
  coupled order conditions, no observed order. That is how F1/F2 got through. Adopt `probe_tableaus.jl`
  (order conditions, cheap) and `probe_imex_stepper.jl` (through the stepper, ~5 s, reuses the existing MUSCL2/RK
  types) into `unit_core.jl`/`unit_schemes.jl`. The "IMEX kinetic relaxation" pipeline test only checks that the runs
  are finite.
- "Implicit stage: RHS from the solve" (`unit_schemes.jl:266`) claims a "second-order IMEX step" with a 5e-3 tolerance
  and no explicit part: it cannot detect an order or coupling defect.
- Implicit source + explicit tableau (F3): untested; `probe_implicit_with_explicit_tableau.jl` as a regression test
  once the behaviour is decided.
- No convergence test on jittered clouds for MUSCL ≥ 2 (all pipeline EOC tests use `Grid_randomness_factor = 0`);
  `probe_global_jitter.jl` shows MUSCL 2–4 converge globally at order on a jittered 1-D cloud (supraconvergent,
  although the pointwise truncation error loses about one order, `probe_rhs_order.jl`), which is worth locking in.
- Central and WENO: no EOC assertion anywhere (pipeline test 1 only checks that the error decreases); WENO's
  smoothness indicators untested (F5). `probe_rhs_order.jl` gives lattice consistency orders (Central2 2.0, Central3
  4.0, WENO2 2.2, WENO3 2.0–3.1) that could be asserted.
- Mass conservation in pipeline test 1 is checked with `rtol = 1e-2` on a uniform periodic lattice, where every
  scheme here should conserve to round-off (antisymmetric weights); `1e-10` would be meaningful.
- `test/pipeline_tests.jl:215`: `@test e1 < max(2e0, 1e-6)` is `e1 < 2` for a discrete mass error, effectively
  vacuous.
- Limiters: no boundedness test of the reconstructions themselves (only the box extrema in the pipeline);
  `probe_limiters.jl` is a cheap direct check (all pass).
- Praveen and Tiwari upwind: only the global 2-D EOC window `0.5 < eoc < 1.5`.

### Minor issues

- `RK2_Ralston_Tableau` docstring "(not SSP)" and the docs table ("Explicit part SSP: no") contradict

### Verdict

The explicit machinery is in good shape: the explicit tableaus are correct (order conditions to round-off, the
SSPRK(5,4) coefficients and `ssp_coefficient` agree with Spiteri–Ruuth, C = 1.50818), the SSP substep MOOD logic is
sound and directly tested, the MLS fits and the MUSCL divergence reach their designed lattice orders and converge
at order on jittered clouds, and the limiters bound the reconstructions. The IMEX side is not: two of the five IMEX
tableaus are wrong (ARS222 is first order on coupled problems, PRSSP3 is inconsistent and badly off in the stiff
limit), both are used by HyperCloud's kinetic/BGK methods and recommended in the docs, and the tests cannot see it.
Fix F1/F2 (with order-condition tests) before relying on any IMEX result; F3–F5 are real but contained; the rest is
hygiene.
